## Privacy language

iCloud protects synchronized data in transit and at rest. Family sharing uses invitations and viewing or editing permissions. Swaddle has no developer-run journal server, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK.

The current implementation does not justify claims of end-to-end encryption, family-only decryption, or inaccessibility to Apple. See [Apple’s iCloud data security overview](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651) and the [Swaddle privacy policy](https://swaddle-ios.app/privacy/).
