A few little questions

Good to know.

Is Swaddle really free?

Yes. Swaddle is completely free, including iCloud family sharing, widgets, Siri actions, growth charts, and milestones. There are no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Can my partner and I share the same log?

Yes. Invite your partner or another caregiver through iCloud using their own Apple Account. Sharing includes every child in the family, care history, pumping, and milestone observations. Choose whether each person can view or edit. iCloud sharing needs an internet connection, and updates can take time.

Where does my family’s information go?

Your journal is stored on your device and uses your iCloud for synchronization and invited family sharing. Swaddle has no separate journal server, advertising, or third-party analytics. Read the privacy policy for Siri, widgets, diagnostics, and your controls.

Does pumping count as my baby’s intake?

No. Pumping records milk expressed by a caregiver. Bottle intake records what your baby drank. Swaddle keeps those totals separate. Breastfeeding is tracked as time, without estimating a milk volume.

Are growth charts and milestones medical advice?

No. WHO growth references and CDC milestone checklists help you organize observations for conversations with your child’s clinician. They do not diagnose conditions or replace a professional developmental screening. CDC and WHO do not endorse Swaddle.

When can I download Swaddle?

Swaddle is being prepared for its App Store launch for iPhone. This page will have a download link when it is available. Already testing Swaddle? Visit Support for help with your current build.
