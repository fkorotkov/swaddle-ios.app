How do I share with my family?
  1. Open Swaddle’s Settings from the gear button.
  2. Go to the family and caregiver controls, then choose to invite or manage caregivers.
  3. Choose view-only or editing access and continue with iCloud.
  4. Send the invitation to the Apple Account your caregiver uses for iCloud. They should open it on their iPhone and finish joining in Swaddle.

If you already created a pumping profile for someone, use Invite this caregiver on that profile to keep their history together.

A shared family includes all its children, care history, shared pumping, and milestone observations. Viewers can read; editors can add, change, and delete shared entries. Each person uses their own Apple Account. An Apple Family Sharing group is not required.

Why aren’t our timelines the same?

Entries save locally first. iCloud then sends and receives changes, so another person’s phone may take time to catch up.

  1. Check that both iPhones have internet access and are signed into iCloud.
  2. Open Swaddle on both devices. Use the cloud status control and Sync now, or pull to refresh the activity list.
  3. Confirm both people are in the same family and viewing the same child. Check that the recipient accepted with the invited Apple Account.
  4. Look for a sync message or error on the phone that created the missing entry as well as the other phone.

Do not delete your family or reinstall as a first troubleshooting step. If it still looks wrong, contact support with the app versions, the time of an affected entry, and the status message. You can choose to include copied sync diagnostics.

How do the feeding, diaper, and pumping totals work?

Today uses the calendar day. Last 24 hours is a rolling window ending now. Chart days use the app’s reporting timezone.

Bottle intake is the milk and formula your baby drank. Pumping is milk expressed by a caregiver and stays separate from intake. Breastfeeding is tracked as time; Swaddle does not estimate a milk volume.

Both is one diaper change that counts as both wet and soiled. Wet plus soiled can therefore be greater than the number of changes.

Volume entry and display use 1 oz = 30 mL. Weight uses a separate kg or lb + oz setting. Changing display units does not create or duplicate entries.

How do I add Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets?

Open Swaddle at least once and select a child first.

Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, open the widget gallery, and search for Swaddle. Choose a summary widget for last-24-hour bottles, family pumping, diapers, and quick actions, or an individual action.

Lock Screen

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, choose Customize, then the Lock Screen. Tap the widget area and choose Swaddle’s Pee, Poo, Both, or Bottle controls.

Diaper controls open Swaddle to save after authentication. Bottle opens the editor so you can review the amount. Quick diaper entries have Undo.

iOS controls widget refresh timing. Check the widget’s “As of” time if its summary seems behind. If Swaddle does not appear in the gallery after an update, open the app again and revisit the gallery.

What should I say to Siri?

Include “in Swaddle” so Siri can choose the app. Try:

  • “Record a bottle in Swaddle.”
  • “Record the usual bottle in Swaddle.”
  • “Record a diaper in Swaddle.” Then answer “Both.”
  • “Log a pee and poo diaper in Swaddle.”
  • “Log expressed milk in Swaddle.”
  • “How much did my baby eat in Swaddle?”
  • “When was the last feed in Swaddle?”

If “pee and poo” is confused with pumping, try “wet and dirty,” or use the two-step diaper phrase. Siri handles speech recognition; Swaddle cannot directly change how Siri hears a word.

Siri may ask for permission, the child, or missing details. A usual bottle is confirmed before saving. Device authentication is required before reading or changing care records. Availability varies with your device, Siri language, and system settings.

You can disable care-history discovery and onscreen record awareness in Swaddle’s Siri settings while keeping basic logging and feeding questions.

What do the growth percentiles mean?

Record the child’s birth date and growth-reference information, then add a dated measurement. Swaddle uses WHO Child Growth Standards for supported ages: weight under 2 years, and length/height and head circumference through the supported range up to 5 years.

A percentile compares a measurement with a reference group of the same age and sex. For example, the 50th percentile is the midpoint of the reference—not a goal. A single percentile does not tell you whether a child is healthy.

For a premature baby, Swaddle can compare the same measurement using actual age since birth and corrected age from the due date. These can produce different percentiles because they use different ages. Corrected-age comparisons require the relevant birth and gestational-age details and a supported age.

If a percentile is unavailable, check those profile details, the measurement date, and whether the measurement is within the reference’s supported age and value range. Tap a chart point for its details.

Growth charts are informational. Discuss the pattern of growth and any concerns with your child’s clinician.

How do milestone check-ins work?

Open the Milestones tab and choose a chapter. Record what you noticed, optionally add the exact first-noticed date, and keep a note. You can return to earlier chapters and look ahead.

Swaddle includes CDC milestone checklists from 2 months through 2 years and WHO motor-development windows. The illustrations show the idea behind an action; they are not a standardized assessment. You can pause animations, and the app respects Reduce Motion.

Children develop at different rates. These observations do not replace developmental screening. If you are concerned or notice a lost skill, speak with your child’s clinician. CDC and WHO do not endorse Swaddle.

Can I import old feeding or pumping records?

Yes. Open More and choose the care-note import option. Paste your notes, then review the proposed records before saving. Check the child or pumping caregiver, timezone, dates, amounts, and any warnings.

For structured data, open the advanced Format tips in the import screen. It documents JSONL: one JSON object per line. Use that version’s documented fields for feeding and pumping records.

Swaddle flags likely duplicates and entries that need more details. Interpretation happens on your device, and saving requires your review.

Which languages and accessibility options are available?

The app supports English, Spanish, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Persian, French, Haitian Creole, Hindi, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Somali, Filipino, Vietnamese, and Russian. Use your iPhone’s app language settings to choose a supported language.

Russian milestone text is a Swaddle translation, not an official CDC Russian translation. Source information is available in the app.

Swaddle supports larger text, light and dark appearances, and Reduce Motion. Milestone illustrations can be paused. Siri features depend on system and language availability, separately from the app’s translated interface.

How can I manage or delete my information?

Entries can be edited or deleted, with history available in the app. Removing a caregiver profile keeps their historical care records. Merging profiles brings attributed history together.

To stop someone’s access, remove the participant in iCloud family access management. Deleting their profile alone does not revoke access.

Permanent family deletion is a separate, confirmed action. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete iCloud records or backups. The current version has no complete bulk export. Read the privacy policy for more about these controls, or contact support for help finding them.
