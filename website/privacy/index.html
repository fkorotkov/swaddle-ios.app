The short version

Your care journal lives on your device and uses Apple’s iCloud for sync and invited family sharing. Swaddle has no separate journal server, ads, or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your family’s information or use it for advertising.

What you choose to record.

Swaddle stores the child and caregiver profiles you create and the care you record. Depending on what you enter, this can include names, birth dates, gestational age, sex or growth-reference settings, feeding, breastfeeding, diapers, pumping, measurements, notes, and milestone observations.

Records may include the caregiver who recorded them, dates and times, edits, and deletion history. The app uses this information to display your journal, calculate summaries, show growth references, and support sharing. You do not need a separate Swaddle account.

On your device. Shared through iCloud.

Your journal is stored locally so you can record care offline. When iCloud is available, Swaddle uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize your family’s information and the records shared with invited caregivers. Swaddle does not upload your journal to a separate server operated by the developer.

Private CloudKit records are not available to the developer through the CloudKit dashboard, and the developer has no general reader account for your family journal. Apple operates iCloud under its own privacy policy and your Apple Account settings. Data protection depends on Apple’s services and configuration; this is not a promise that every field is end-to-end encrypted.

The people you invite

Family sharing includes the family’s current and future children, care history, shared pumping, milestone observations, and change history. You choose whether invited participants can view or edit. Editors can add, change, or delete shared entries. Personal entry defaults stay separate from the shared family log.

Remove a participant in family access management to stop their shared access. Deleting a caregiver profile alone does not revoke their iCloud access. A recipient may retain information they already viewed or copied. Synchronization and access changes can take time to reach other devices.

Siri, search, and widgets.

When you use Siri, Apple handles the voice request according to your device’s Siri and privacy settings. Swaddle’s care actions require device authentication before reading or changing the journal.

On supported systems, care-history discovery makes records available to Siri and system search and can provide context about the record onscreen. You can turn this off in Swaddle’s Siri settings. Basic voice logging and feeding questions remain available.

Widgets use a local shared app cache for summaries and quick actions. Names and totals may be visible on the surfaces where you place widgets. Swaddle supports the system’s privacy redaction, and your iPhone’s Lock Screen settings also affect what is visible.

Interpreting care notes.

Pasted care notes are interpreted on your device. Where supported and available, Apple’s on-device language model may help interpret a short unstructured note. Structured JSONL import uses a local parser. You review the proposed records before saving them.

Swaddle does not send pasted care notes to a developer-hosted AI service.

When you ask for help.

If you contact support, the developer receives what you voluntarily send: your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostic information you include. This information is used to answer your request, investigate a problem, and maintain the app. It is not used for advertising or to train an AI model.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any related follow-up or legal obligation. You can request deletion using the contact below. Please remove names and care details that are not needed, and never send an Apple Account password or verification code.

Apple may provide crash reports or diagnostics through its services according to your sharing choices and Apple’s policies. Swaddle has no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. The statistics inside the app are calculated for your family’s use.

Your information. Your controls.

You can view and edit your journal and review edits and deleted entries in the app. Some deletion actions preserve recoverable history; removing or merging a caregiver profile preserves the care attributed to that person.

Permanent family deletion is a separate action with an explicit confirmation. Read that confirmation carefully. Shared changes may take time to reach other devices. Uninstalling Swaddle alone does not delete iCloud records, revoke another caregiver’s access, or remove information from device backups.

Apple Account and device settings control iCloud and backups. The current app does not offer a complete bulk export. Support can explain available controls, but cannot retrieve a private family journal from a developer server because there is no such journal server.

Made for adult caregivers.

Swaddle is designed for adults to keep a care journal about children. Children do not need an account, and the app does not create public child profiles, a social feed, or advertising profiles.

About this website.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, with domain services provided by Cloudflare. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and pages requested to deliver and secure their services. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

We do not add advertising cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or a mailing-list form to this site. Website visits do not give it access to your Swaddle journal. External links and your email provider operate under their own policies.

Questions, requests, and updates.

Swaddle is provided by Fedor Korotkov. For support or privacy requests, contact support@swaddle-ios.app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information you have sent to support; use the app and Apple’s controls for your device and iCloud journal.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.
